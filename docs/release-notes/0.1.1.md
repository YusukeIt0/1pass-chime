- The volume slider shows what it means: tick marks at each quarter, "Quieter" on the left and "Mac volume" on the right, and the current level as a percentage. The right end plays at your Mac's output volume; the app can't play louder than that.
- New installs start at the Mac's volume (100%). A volume you already set is kept.

Builds are not notarized by Apple yet; the installer checks the checksum and code signature.
